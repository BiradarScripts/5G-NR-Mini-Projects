# Frequency analysis note

The raw chronological plots are supplemented with two normalized measures:

1. occurrences per 10,000 words, calculated as total term occurrences divided by total words in the period, multiplied by 10,000; and
2. occurrences per report, calculated as total term occurrences divided by the number of reports in the period.

Because the metadata do not include a complete count of instruments reviewed in each report, per-instrument normalization is not reported by default; add `num_instruments` to the metadata CSV to enable it.

The decade bins follow the manuscript's corpus periods: 1954-1963, 1964-1973, ..., 2014-2023.
